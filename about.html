About Facility Hubs

One central place for everything your business needs

Facility Hubs creates customized internal business tools that bring your resources, information, and everyday processes together in one secure, easy-to-use location.

Every hub is password protected and limited to your organization only

Small businesses often rely on a patchwork of spreadsheets, shared drives, payment links, forms, email threads, and disconnected software. As the business grows, finding the right information becomes harder, and simple tasks begin taking more time than they should.

Facility Hubs provides a clearer way to work.

We build tailored digital hubs that give your team one central place to access the tools and information they use every day. Each hub is designed around your business, your workflows, and the people who need to use it.

What can a Facility Hub do?

Your hub can be designed to support a wide range of internal operations, including:

  • Employee and contractor directories
  • Company documents and shared resources
  • Policies, procedures, and training materials
  • Payment portals and financial links
  • Vendor and supplier directories
  • Client or member information
  • Internal request and intake forms
  • Staff onboarding resources
  • Scheduling and booking links
  • Inventory and equipment records
  • Property or facility information
  • Project dashboards and status updates
  • Frequently asked questions
  • Announcements and team updates
  • Quick access to commonly used software
  • Brand assets, templates, and marketing materials

Whether you need a simple internal directory or a more complete business operations hub, the goal is the same: make important information easier to find, manage, and use.

Built for small businesses

Facility Hubs is designed for businesses that need better internal organization without the complexity, cost, or learning curve of a large enterprise system.

Instead of asking your team to adapt to rigid software, we create a practical solution that reflects how your business already works. Your hub can begin with the essentials and expand as your needs change.

The result is less time spent searching, fewer disconnected processes, and a more consistent experience for your team.
